Problem Statement
The Multi-Vector Fraud Threat in UPI
🚨 The Social Engineering Dilemma
UPI processes over 13 billion transactions monthly. Fraudsters now use psychological coercion to convince victims into authorizing payments themselves.
🔍 Common Attack Vectors:
• Voice Phishing (Vishing): Fake bank managers & police officials.
• Urgent KYC Coercion: "Your account will be frozen in 10 minutes".
• Credential Theft: Fake refund links requesting OTP / PIN.
• Remote Access Traps: AnyDesk, TeamViewer screen sharing.
Blindspot of Traditional Systems
Legacy payment gateways evaluate only amounts and static thresholds after or during execution.

To legacy systems, a ₹40,000 transfer initiated by a panicked victim looks 100% syntactically valid.

FraudShield solves this by analyzing pre-execution context across 5 signal layers.

Voice Shield
Voice Phishing & Social Engineering Shield
🎙️ In-Memory Acoustic & NLP Scanner
• Authority Impersonation: Detects claims of RBI, Police, or Bank Manager.
• Extreme Urgency: Flags "Within 10 mins", "Account blocked", "Arrest warrant".
• Credential Theft: Intercepts requests for 6-digit OTP, MPIN, CVV.
• Remote Access Traps: AnyDesk, TeamViewer, QuickSupport.
🔒 Privacy-by-Design Safeguards
• Zero Persistent Audio: Waveforms processed in volatile memory and immediately discarded.
• Token-Only Scanning: Evaluates only scam intent phrases; ignores private dialogue.
• DPDP Act 2023 Compliant: Minimizes personal data exposure.
Device & Behavioral
Hardware Fingerprints & Behavioral Profiling
📱 Device Trust Scoring (0–100)
• Generates hardware signature hashes based on Model, OS, Browser, and Resolution.
• Trusted (70–100): Known primary smartphone with long-term history.
• Untrusted / Flagged (0–30): Unfamiliar device or shared across multiple victim accounts.
📊 Dynamic Behavioral Baselines
• Learns personalized normal spending windows (e.g. 8 AM – 10 PM).
• Tracks historical average amounts and transaction velocity.
• Flags sudden 8.0x multipliers or rapid consecutive failure bursts.
